## Deliberate deviations in proof route

- **Negative correction.** The report discards `-F` by positivity. The Lean proof bounds it in norm, paying an extra `2(r-1)`. The collected bound becomes `d + 3p + ps + ts`, which `small_row_cost_le` still fits under `d + 27q³`. The final constant is unchanged.
- **Fixed-size sampling.** Section 10 uses continuous priorities, two Bernoulli thresholds and Chernoff bounds (10.4)-(10.8). None of that exists in Lean. Instead a finite nested fill/thin coupling plus conditional Jensen on the convex map `A ↦ tr(A^{2q})` gives the fixed-size moment at most `4^{2q}` times the Bernoulli moment (`Sampling.bernoulli_trace_moment_transfer`). The 8192 constant absorbs the factor 4 through `amplified_envelope_le_half`. This is documented in `notes/finite_jensen_sampling.md` and is a different proof of the same Theorem 1.
- **Leverage parameter.** The report tracks `μ = max ℓ_i` throughout Sections 3 to 5. Lean sets `μ` to its upper bound 1 everywhere, which is all Theorem 1 needs.

## Not formalized or weaker than stated

- **Equation (11.5).** The report claims `M = O(ε⁻²[d + log³(1/δ)])`. The Lean result is `M ≤ (8192(1+432/δ)+1)·d/ε²`, which is linear in `d` but polynomial rather than logarithmic in `1/δ`. The README states the weaker form correctly. The tex asymptotic is not a proved theorem in this project.
- **Appendix A.** The support-alignment obstruction, (A.2)-(A.4) and A.4, has no Lean counterpart anywhere in the tree.
- **Theorem 2 for general `ρ`.** Only `ρ = M/n` is stated, as noted above.

## Minor statement-level differences that do not weaken anything

- The Lean failure event is `ε ≤ ‖G_M − I‖`, which is larger than the report's `> ε`, so the Lean bound is slightly stronger.
- `two_round_srht_ose` carries an explicit `d ≤ 2^k` hypothesis. The report derives it from `UᵀU = I`. Redundant but harmless.
- `failureOrder` is defined via `logb 4`; `failureOrder_original` proves it equals the report's `⌈log(4d/δ)/(2 log 2)⌉`.
- Rows are an unordered `Finset` of size `M` rather than an ordered selection. The Gram and norm conclusions are identical.
- The `k = 0` and `M = n` endpoints are covered by the full-row branch, as the report requires.
